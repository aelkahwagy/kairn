Made for iPhone Duo

Unfold your workout.

Open iPhone Duo and Kairn puts the exercise on one side and big, easy controls on the other. Navigation moves into the strip beside the status bar, so the whole screen goes to your training. Prop it up for a hands-free view you can read across the room.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your plan, workouts and voice stay on your iPhone. The coach uses Apple Intelligence on device where available, and Kairn saves to Apple Health only if you choose.
