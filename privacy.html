Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Kairn is built so your training data stays with you.

What we collect

Nothing. Kairn has no accounts, no analytics service, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your name (if you add one), training preferences, plan and workout history are stored only on your device. The AI coach runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device models; your messages are not sent to us or to any third party.

Voice coach

If you use it, Kairn uses the microphone only while voice mode is open. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone with on-device recognition, and replies are spoken by your iPhone. Audio and transcripts are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere. On devices that can't recognise speech on the device, voice mode stays off.

Apple Health

Only if you switch it on. Kairn saves finished strength workouts and an estimated energy value to Apple Health, and reads your body weight to make that estimate. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone, and never leaves your iPhone through Kairn. You can turn access off anytime in Settings › Health.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you create a share card and choose where to send it. You decide which details appear on the card.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete all data removes everything Kairn stored. Deleting the app does the same.

Health information

Kairn is a fitness app, not a medical service. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Children

Kairn is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
